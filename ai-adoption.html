01 · RESEARCH QUESTION

AI adoption is not one financial event.

The paper asks how the launch and scaling of Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses changed EssilorLuxottica’s financial profile between 2023 and 2024.

Instead of treating adoption as a binary “success,” the analysis follows three connected signals: revenue growth, operating-cost pressure, and sustained investment in innovation.

07 · LIMITATIONS

What this case can—and cannot—show.

The study focuses on one market-leading company over a short period. It is useful for tracing mechanisms and financial trade-offs, but it cannot establish that AI adoption alone caused the observed changes.

A stronger next step would compare multiple traditional manufacturers, extend the time horizon, and separate product-specific investment from company-wide changes.
